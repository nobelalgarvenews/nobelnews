Nobel Algarve News is Officially Live

Monday, October 5th, 2026




Welcome to the Nobel Algarve News! This website was made to help us students have a better idea of what’s going on in our school. Our school is pretty big, and it is not easy for information to reach all students purely by word of mouth. I, for one, often find out about school events only when they are taking place. Being constantly out of the loop can get pretty frustrating, to be honest, so I wanted to create a remedy.

More than that, however, this is a place for our opinions to be expressed on an open platform. Our goal is not to project an ultra-curated image of the school, but to reflect students’ views in an honest way. The primary focus of Nobel Algarve News will always be to represent students faithfully.

A keen-eyed viewer may have noticed the ‘Submissions’ section of this website. That’s right, the articles uploaded by the NAN may be on a topic submitted by a student. The subject does not have to be too serious. If you have a gripe about the school, a project you want to promote, or anything you want to bring attention to— submit it to us with sufficient detail. Our team will take a look and see what can be written on the matter.

Thank you for visiting our website, and we hope to see you again soon!
